# 压力影响 (Stress Influence)

## Overview

Stress Influence refers to how under high pressure, humans enter "fight or flight" mode, with cognitive ability decreasing and more reliance on本能 and habits. Almost all cognitive biases intensify under stress. Stress influence illustrates the adverse effects of extreme situations on decision quality. Munger quotes: "When the battle is intense, you don't rise to the level of expectation, you sink to the level of training." Therefore, basic skills and processes should be practiced well in peacetime to avoid mistakes under pressure. It also reminds leaders that when teams are under high pressure, adjusting atmosphere and reducing pressure is important, otherwise erroneous decisions and conflict probabilities increase sharply. Individuals also need to learn methods to remain calm under stress.

## When to Apply

- Emergency management: Through drills and contingency plans, enable people to follow procedures in real crises without being flustered under pressure
- Team: When teams are at high-pressure nodes (projects racing against deadlines, declining performance), managers should pay attention to members' psychological states, provide support to reduce excessive pressure, prevent collapse or internal friction
- Personal: Master stress management skills (breathing relaxation, positive self-talk, etc.), and try to avoid making major decisions in extremely tense states; if emotions are bad, can delay decisions or seek旁观者 opinions

## How to Use

1. Practice basic procedures until they become automatic
2. Develop stress management techniques in advance
3. Create contingency plans before crises occur
4. Build in decision delays for high-stakes situations
5. Support team members during high-pressure periods

## Examples

### Example 1: Firefighter Training
Firefighters who haven't undergone strict training, when actually facing a big fire, often panic from fear; those well-trained will act automatically according to procedures under pressure, reducing panic and mistakes.

### Example 2: Corporate Crisis Management
When companies encounter crises (public criticism), management is under tremendous pressure and easily engages in blame-shifting, mutual accusations, or hasty decisions. If contingency plans and calm minds exist in peacetime, larger errors can be avoided.

### Example 3: Test Anxiety
Test anxiety is typical: some students can solve problems normally but under exam tension, their minds go blank—this is stress impairing cognitive function.

## Core Principles

- Under stress, cognitive ability decreases significantly
- "You sink to the level of training" under pressure
- Almost all cognitive biases intensify under stress
- Basic skills and processes practiced in peacetime protect against errors
- Reducing pressure improves decision quality

## Warning Signs / Common Mistakes / Benefits

- Warning: High stress impairs judgment and increases errors
- Warning: Crisis situations amplify all cognitive biases
- Mistake: Making major decisions under extreme stress
- Mistake: Not preparing procedures and training in advance
- Benefit: Well-practiced procedures become automatic under stress
- Benefit: Stress management skills improve performance under pressure
