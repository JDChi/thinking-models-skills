# 否认倾向 (Denial)

## Overview

Denial is a defense mechanism where humans often choose to deny or ignore painful realities or obvious warning signs to reduce psychological discomfort. This is one of the defense mechanisms that allows people to escape truths that must be faced. Denial provides short-term self-comfort but is harmful in the long term. Munger lists it as one of the roots of stupidity: many companies in the process of collapse have management denying the severity of the problem. Understanding this tendency can help us forcibly break denial when crisis signs appear in ourselves or organizations, such as bravely facing financial losses or health problems. Managers should also create a culture that encourages speaking truth and prevents subordinates from hiding bad news due to fear or denial.

## When to Apply

- Decision-making: Regularly reflect, "Am I avoiding a fact I don't want to face?" When necessary, seek external review to break cognitive blind spots
- Enterprise: Establish early warning mechanisms; when key indicators deteriorate to a threshold, management must take it seriously and formulate correction plans, not deceive themselves
- Psychology: Cultivate the courage to face pain, such as daring to look at financial accounts and dare to listen to criticism, because admitting a problem is the first step to solving it

## How to Use

1. Create systems that surface bad news rather than suppress it
2. Reward people who bring problems to light early
3. Regular self-reflection about what you're avoiding
4. Seek external perspectives when internal denial is likely
5. Accept that facing problems early is less painful than facing them late

## Examples

### Example 1: Health Symptoms
Some people, when serious symptoms appear, are too scared to seek medical examination due to fear, tricking themselves that "it's nothing." This is denial that may delay the best treatment timing.

### Example 2: Project Delays and Overruns
When a company project repeatedly delays and exceeds budget, the project manager reports to superiors that "everything is normal." This is denial psychology at work, unwilling to admit failure signs.

### Example 3: Boiling Frog Metaphor
The famous "boiling frog" metaphor illustrates that if deterioration is gradual, people will continue to deny danger until it's too late.

## Core Principles

- Denial is short-term self-comfort but long-term harmful
- Many corporate collapses happen with management denying problems
- Admitting a problem is the first step to solving it
- Organizational culture should encourage speaking truth
- "Ostrich mentality" of burying head in sand is dangerous

## Warning Signs / Common Mistakes / Benefits

- Warning: Denial delays problem-solving and makes crises worse
- Warning: Cultural suppression of bad news enables organizational denial
- Mistake: Ignoring warning signs hoping they will go away
- Mistake: Punishing people who bring bad news
- Benefit: Early problem recognition enables early intervention
- Benefit: Truth-telling culture improves organizational resilience
