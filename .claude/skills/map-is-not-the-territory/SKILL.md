# 地图非领土 (Map Is Not the Territory)

## Overview

This metaphor comes from Alfred Korzybski. Any model, theory, or description ("the map") is merely a simplification of reality and not equivalent to reality itself ("the territory"). If a map were to represent the territory with complete accuracy, it would be as large as the territory itself, rendering it useless.

## When to Apply

- Making decisions using any model or data
- Evaluating predictive or analytical results
- Managing based on KPIs or metrics
- Facing expert opinions or theories
- When actual results don't match model predictions

## How to Use

1. **Acknowledge simplification**: Understand that any model is a simplification of reality
2. **Identify assumptions**: Know the assumptions and boundary conditions behind the model
3. **Maintain humility**: Don't blindly trust models while ignoring reality
4. **Trust reality**: When data conflicts with intuition, prioritize trusting reality
5. **Continuously修正**: Adjust models based on actual circumstances

## Examples

### Example 1: KPI Management
- Map: Customer service satisfaction scores
- Territory: Actual customer service quality
- Problem: Employees may overcompensate to meet metrics, harming company interests
- Solution: Combine multiple metrics and regularly verify actual situations

### Example 2: Financial Models
- Map: Credit rating models
- Territory: Actual bond risk
- Problem: During the 2008 financial crisis, many highly-rated products had enormous actual risk
- Lesson: Disconnect between model assumptions and reality can lead to disaster

### Example 3: Economic Forecasting
- Map: GDP growth forecast models
- Territory: Actual economic conditions
- Problem: Models cannot predict the impact of unexpected events (like pandemics)
- Solution: Remain cautious about model results and leave buffer space

## Core Principles

- **Models have value but limits**: Models are useful but not reality itself
- **Assumptions are not facts**: Model assumptions may not hold in reality
- **Trust reality**: When reality conflicts with models, believe reality
- **Stay updated**: Continuously修正 models based on new data

## Warning Signs

- Over-reliance on a single metric
- Ignoring differences between model assumptions and reality
- Treating model predictions as certain outcomes
- Refusing to accept evidence that doesn't match the model
